# Groundedness Ladder — v0.8 Economy

## Grounded now

- deterministic offline runtime;
- actual resident records as customers and workers;
- real commercial place occupancy;
- explicit deposits, recurring costs, money, wages, and service records;
- bounded enterprise templates and need signals;
- compressed and interactive work;
- equipment identity, wear, repair, and upgrades;
- optional lifecycle and no age pressure;
- autonomous minority enterprise formation;
- export, schemas, validation, and exact replay.

## Plausible later on this foundation

- product-level inventory with source provenance;
- deeper cooperative agreements;
- storefront interiors and exterior identity;
- resident-created product/service variations;
- suppliers and local production chains;
- business inheritance and succession;
- player/NPC partnerships with explicit ownership shares;
- bounded local AI proposals validated by the deterministic engine.

## Speculative

- emergent multi-town economies with independently evolving cultures;
- local AI residents designing novel services from open needs;
- cross-game enterprises that export deterministic goods or histories;
- real-world creator revenue attribution derived from in-game provenance.

Speculative items are not implemented claims and should not be represented as current capability.
