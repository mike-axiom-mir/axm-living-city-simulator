# Ordinary Encounters and Compression Parity

## Ordinary encounter

An encounter is a small possibility created by grounded shared-space overlap. It is not a quest, checklist, streak, reputation gate, or compulsory greeting.

Responses:

- **Greet:** explicit five-minute action with bounded social effect.
- **Quiet acknowledgment:** truthful response; no time, money, needs, or relationship mutation.
- **Not now:** truthful refusal; no time, money, needs, or relationship mutation.
- **Pass:** the moment ends without penalty.

Decline must not create an empty relationship record or any hidden structural residue beyond the encounter history itself.

## Movement parity

Visible, compressed, and compress-remainder movement preserve:

- route;
- access basis;
- elapsed/remaining minutes;
- stair use;
- destination;
- final presence;
- authority.

Visible mode may expose atmosphere or an optional encounter. It may not grant superior rewards. Compressed mode removes watching, not consequences or reality.
