# Casual Exterior Roots — No Façade Chores v0.10.0

## Why this contract exists

Adding shells, roofs, windows, stairs, and frontages creates a predictable simulation-design risk: realism can become a list of repetitive obligations.

AXM Living City does not treat “more systems” as “more chores.” Exterior depth exists to make places feel persistent, expressive, and lived—not to demand that the player repaint, clean, inspect, and optimize every property on a schedule.

## Authoritative roots

- No façade maintenance streak.
- No daily frontage decay.
- No universal exterior quality score.
- No one statistically correct appearance.
- No reward for checking the Buildings tab every day.
- No punishment for ignoring a resident request until it reaches its stated outcome.
- No forced business/property endgame.
- No age deadline for renovating, moving, starting, pausing, or caring.
- No travel or visible-animation superiority.

## Realism that remains

Casual does not mean consequence-free or fake.

- A frontage project has an exact cost.
- The resident must have and save real money.
- Authority can approve or refuse.
- Work has real phases.
- A malformed change can fail validation and roll back.
- Ownership and occupancy remain separate.
- A resident may later move for ordinary life reasons.
- The shell retains history and identity.

The compression target is repetitive servicing, not causality.

## Future merge gate

Any future weather, damage, accessibility, construction, inspection, or neighborhood-appearance layer must answer all of these before merge:

1. Does the mechanic create mandatory recurring clicks?
2. Can the consequence be resolved through compressed, delegated, or optional paths?
3. Is a player who does not engage being labeled irresponsible or behind?
4. Does ownership become control over residents?
5. Does one exterior build become statistically mandatory?
6. Does the change preserve place, object, resident, and source identity?
7. Is the state visible and explainable?
8. Can refusal, pause, and release remain valid?
9. Is age pressure still false?
10. Does local export preserve the full evidence?

A “realistic” system that fails these roots is not automatically a good addition.
