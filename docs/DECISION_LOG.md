# Decision Log — v0.9.0

## D-090-01: Physical continuity before another management layer

The next branch after local economy was street/exterior continuity, not deeper business accounting.

## D-090-02: Compression parity is a protected root

Visible travel and compressed travel use the same route and costs. Watching animation cannot become the optimal strategy.

## D-090-03: No hidden mood reward

An early visible-travel implementation improved mood. That was removed because it silently pressured visible walking.

## D-090-04: Street moments remain observation-only

The city may reveal life without turning every observation into an event prompt, relationship, or reward.

## D-090-05: Resident movement uses multiple resolutions

Player-selected routes may be minute-level. Resident schedules retain route evidence without continuous animation.

## D-090-06: Current infrastructure may be derived; past behavior may not

Migration may derive present exteriors and a current graph from existing places. It may not fabricate prior journeys.

## D-090-07: Performance cache stays outside world state

Pure route results may be cached in a runtime `WeakMap`. Cache state is not simulation truth.

## D-090-08: Exterior identity is place-bound

Address and frontage identity survive occupant and ownership changes. Authority to edit remains separately scoped.
