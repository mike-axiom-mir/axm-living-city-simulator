# Presence Privacy and Authority

## Core rule

**Simulation knowledge is not automatic player visibility.**

The deterministic engine may need to know enough to update schedules and continuity. The interface exposes exact presence only where the player could lawfully know it through co-presence or a public/shared context.

## Disclosure levels

- Public threshold or public place: exact place may be shown.
- Shared building route: exact landing/storey may be shown when lawfully co-present.
- Shared/common room: exact room may be shown when lawfully co-present.
- Private interior elsewhere: only coarse “inside private home” state, no room ID.

## Ownership boundary

Owning a property occupied by a tenant does not grant private entry or remote presence visibility.

## Visit boundary

A bounded access grant may permit shared-space entry. It does not grant tenancy, storage, object ownership, editing, construction, household/family/care authority, employment, or surveillance.

## Validation roots

The world is invalid when:

- remote private-room visibility is enabled;
- presence surveillance is enabled;
- watching creates rewards;
- continuous room tracking is required;
- a presence/access record claims wider authority;
- room/place/storey references are structurally invalid.

Validation diagnoses and does not repair.
