# Household Agreement Engine — v0.2 foundation, v0.3 integration

The Household Agreement Engine remains the authority organ for commitment, cohabitation, shared finance, space, relocation, renovation, goals, conflict/repair, and unilateral exit. v0.3 does not replace it; Structural Habitats consumes its accepted authority.

## Core rule

A partner remains an autonomous person. A proposal is a request, acceptance is recorded consent, and neither transfers direct control or object ownership.

## Relationship path

- dating/relationship state;
- commitment proposal with delayed deterministic response;
- accepted agreement with separate accounts;
- separate cohabitation proposal tied to a real home;
- active shared-home terms;
- renegotiation, initiative, conflict, repair, or unilateral exit.

## v0.3 structural integration

The agreement’s space branch now contains graph room permissions and `roomGraphRevision`. Legacy rectangular zones remain for migration evidence.

A structural renovation proposal stores:

- kind `structural_project`;
- exact project specification;
- property ID;
- readable summary.

Acceptance calls the Habitat Engine to create an authorized phased project. Structure remains unchanged until work completes.

## Boundaries preserved

- household consent cannot invent property ownership;
- owner consent cannot replace household consent;
- partner-private room remains protected;
- common-room change can require both people;
- declining does not apply a hidden punishment flag;
- ending the agreement requires no partner approval;
- no automatic eviction or object transfer on separation.

## Current scope

One player plus one adult autonomous partner. Multi-party households require capability/decision-rule permissions rather than additional hardcoded rectangles.
