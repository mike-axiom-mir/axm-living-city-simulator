# Enterprise Authority and Actual Customers

## Authority layers

v0.8 keeps these separate:

- enterprise ownership;
- personal-project authorship;
- equipment ownership;
- commercial-room occupancy;
- property ownership;
- customer participation;
- bounded work agreement;
- household membership;
- family care authority;
- construction permission;
- community connection.

No layer inherits another automatically.

## Owner authority

An owner may change the enterprise path, pricing, room, equipment investment, session mode, pause state, pivot, withdrawal, or closure when the record grants that authority.

Ownership does not permit control over a worker’s home, relationships, schedule outside the agreement, personal money, or life direction.

## Premise authority

Leasing a commercial room grants occupancy to one enterprise under explicit costs. It does not transfer the underlying place to the owner and does not create residential tenancy, construction authority, or control over neighboring places.

## Customer authority

A resident becomes a customer only inside a settled session. Their record is used to determine eligibility and affordability. Their payment and service outcome are recorded.

Customer status grants no:

- friendship;
- romance;
- household membership;
- dependency;
- recurring subscription;
- visibility ranking;
- obligation to return;
- ownership transfer beyond an explicitly modeled transaction.

There is no anonymous “foot traffic” record capable of paying money.

## Worker offers

A worker offer contains:

- enterprise ID;
- person ID;
- wage per session;
- bounded sessions per week;
- due day and delayed response;
- status and response evidence;
- sessions worked and wages.

Sending the offer creates no immediate work. Acceptance grants enterprise-session participation only. It does not grant ownership, tenancy, household, family, construction, or general schedule authority.

## Wages

Before a worker joins a session, wage capacity is reserved. Settlement pays the exact recorded wage and attributes it to the enterprise. Cancelling returns unused reserve.

The system cannot quietly make a resident work for free because the business lacks money.

## Refusal and exit

Declining or ending work preserves the existing relationship state. The event remains in history, but there is no hidden friendship, trust, romance, housing, or reputation punishment.

## Source integrity

Where an enterprise materially grows from a personal project, `sourceProjectId` remains attached. Pivoting or closing does not erase that source. This is a technical provenance base, not yet a full revenue-sharing or intellectual-property engine.
