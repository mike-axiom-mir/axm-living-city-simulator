# AXM Reusable Organs — Living City v0.11.0

## Building-shell organs retained from v0.10

### Deterministic Place-to-Building Grouper

Assigns every place to exactly one persistent shell while allowing multiple places to share one building without merging tenancy, interiors, objects, or authority.

### Low-Graphic Storey Envelope

Represents each storey through stable corner nodes, exterior wall edges, and typed openings. It is lightweight, deterministic, schema-validatable, and suitable for later visual layers.

### Vertical Continuity Router

Connects street access, landings, stairs, and unit entries. Proves upper-floor reachability without simulating every physical step.

### Interior/Exterior Continuity Bridge

Links address and external door evidence to the existing structural habitat entry room while explicitly granting no new authority.

### Exact Bounded Proposal Organ

Requires a proposed mutation to name its target, before value, after value, author, owner authority, cost, and status before any state changes.

### Proposal-Is-Not-Construction Gate

Separates idea, authority decision, funding, phased work, final mutation, and validation. Reusable for property, vehicle, public-space, equipment, and collaborative changes.

### Resident-Funded Microproject Escrow

Moves attributable resident money into a scoped project without magical funding or silent owner charges.

### Post-Mutation Snapshot Validator

Snapshots linked records, applies one exact mutation, validates cross-record invariants, and restores the snapshot on failure while preserving failed-attempt evidence.

### Refusal-Neutral Authority Response

Records a valid refusal without altering an unrelated relationship or labeling the author’s preference as failure.

### No-Chore Capability Flags

Makes absence of maintenance pressure, daily decay, and optimization scoring explicit and machine-verifiable rather than merely promised in design prose.

### Isolated Shell Random Stream

Allows autonomous frontage timing and owner decisions without shifting family, economy, housing, community, or travel outcomes.
