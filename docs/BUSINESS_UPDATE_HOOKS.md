# Business Update Hooks — Fulfilled and Remaining

This document originally protected future business integration points. v0.8 fulfills the first layer while retaining remaining gates.

## Fulfilled in v0.8

- Personal projects can remain private or become enterprise provenance.
- Existing resident needs form inspectable local-need signals.
- Commercial places have real availability and occupancy.
- Equipment uses repair and upgrade without replacement.
- Work can be compressed or entered.
- Customers are existing autonomous residents.
- Workers receive delayed bounded offers and attributable wages.
- Pause, pivot, room release, and closure preserve history.
- Autonomous residents can form and operate a minority of enterprises.
- Economy randomness is isolated from unrelated deterministic branches.

## Still deliberately deferred

- product-level inventory and provenance;
- suppliers and production chains;
- exterior storefront construction;
- cooperative ownership shares and governance;
- taxes, permits, insurance, loans, debt, insolvency, and legal disputes;
- full labor schedules, leave, negotiation, safety, and employment-law simulation;
- business inheritance;
- multi-location operations;
- online trade or hosted economy;
- AI-generated enterprise concepts.

## Merge gate for later business growth

A later layer should be rejected or branched if it:

- makes business the universal endgame;
- rewards daily engagement or punishes absence;
- creates anonymous customers or magical money;
- treats workers as owned units;
- makes one layout, price, or scale objectively mandatory;
- erases source-project or equipment provenance;
- introduces age pressure;
- silently broadens property, household, or social authority;
- removes local export or deterministic validation.
