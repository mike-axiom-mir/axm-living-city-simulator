# Community Authority, Invitations, and Visits

## Core rule

A social connection is not an authority grant.

The simulation separates:

- knowing somebody;
- trusting somebody;
- attending the same event;
- visiting a home;
- living in a home;
- editing a home;
- storing objects there;
- employing somebody;
- caring for a dependent;
- joining an adult household.

These states may relate, but one cannot silently create another.

## Invitation flow

```text
person forms intention
→ exact invitation record
→ recipient decides
→ accepted or declined evidence
→ event exists only if accepted
```

Sending an invitation does not move anybody or create access.

## Decline rule

Declining an invitation:

- does not spend activity money;
- does not alter player needs or skills;
- does not reduce friendship, trust, romance, or community connection values;
- does not create a reputation penalty;
- remains visible as a respected outcome.

A future narrative system may allow visible human reactions, but it may not smuggle in a universal punishment for boundaries.

## Home visit authority flags

Every home-visit opportunity explicitly carries false authority for:

```text
tenancy
edit
storage
employment
care
```

Construction authority is also absent.

During a visit, a person may be physically present at the host location. Their legal home, tenancy, belongings, household membership, and future access remain unchanged.

## Membership authority

Joining an institution grants participation eligibility. It does not grant:

- compulsory attendance;
- control over other members;
- access to their homes;
- permanent mentor authority;
- employment;
- care or guardianship;
- property rights.

## Waiting-state rule

A waitlist is evidence of real capacity. It must not become:

- paid priority;
- artificial scarcity;
- a fear-of-missing-out timer;
- hidden ranking;
- punishment for withdrawal.

When a place opens, promotion follows deterministic queue evidence.

## Validation

The engine validates authority flags and participant state. Invalid imported authority is diagnosed. It is not silently converted into a legal visit or relationship.
