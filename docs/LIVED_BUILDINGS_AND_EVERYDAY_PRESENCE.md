# Lived Buildings and Everyday Presence

## Purpose

Make the already persistent streets, shells, storeys, stairs, units, rooms, and residents feel connected without creating surveillance, social chores, or minute-by-minute simulation cost.

## Authoritative flow

```text
current lawful location
→ threshold or current room
→ exact access decision
→ existing shell/habitat route
→ visible, compressed, or schedule resolution
→ updated current presence
→ bounded retained evidence
```

Street travel and indoor access remain separate. Reaching an address never means entering it.

## Current snapshot

Each person receives one current snapshot containing only valid references for their state. Autonomous private-home presence may deliberately omit the exact room.

## Indoor routes

- Arrival follows street access → landing → stairs if needed → unit entry → habitat entry room.
- Departure reverses that route.
- Room transition follows actual habitat door connectivity and permissions.
- Visible and compressed execution share the same route and cost.
- Schedule-scale NPC movement resolves the same type of evidence without player-facing animation tax.

## Authority

Presence and routes are spatial facts. They grant no wider authority. A person may be present as a visitor, resident, worker, participant, customer, or passerby without any of those roles collapsing into another.

## Retention

Current snapshots remain authoritative. Detailed movement history is bounded so the simulation does not grow without limit. Ledger summaries preserve meaningful evidence.
