AXM LIVING CITY — INTERIOR FEEDBACK STEWARD PASS v0.11.3

FASTEST START
1. Open: standalone/AXM_LIVING_CITY_SIM_INTERIOR_FEEDBACK_v0_11_3.html
2. The game opens in Living View and Room mode.
3. Browse rooms, focus a persistent object, and choose a grounded item under Things to do here.
4. After success, inspect What actually changed and the focused-object details.
5. Use Open in Build & Home to continue with that real object.
6. Compare Full, Gentle, and Still motion, then inspect Building, Street, or Follow presence.

NO INSTALLATION OR INTERNET IS REQUIRED.

TRUTH BOUNDARY
- The ordinary activity engine applies the action once.
- The feedback card reports measured changes; it creates none.
- A completed echo is after-the-fact, not current presence.
- Room browsing and object focus do not move the player or widen authority.
- Older v0.11.2 receipts may have no observed deltas; the UI must not invent them.

ROOTS TO PRESERVE
- one controlled life and autonomous residents;
- casual realism without chores or age pressure;
- presence is evidence, not authority;
- private rooms are not remotely exposed;
- visible and compressed paths remain equally valid;
- no watching reward, routine checklist, or compulsory greeting;
- object identity, scoped authority, diagnostic-only validation, and local export remain intact.
